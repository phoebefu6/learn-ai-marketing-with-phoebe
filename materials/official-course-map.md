# Official course map - learn-ai-marketing-with-phoebe

**Course:** AI + Marketing - how marketers embrace AI across the funnel
**Scope:** Broad marketing function, both B2B and B2C: research, positioning/messaging, content, SEO/GEO, paid ads + creative, email/lifecycle, social/short-form, visuals, analytics/optimization, governance. EXCLUDES deep measurement/attribution modeling (that is the sibling course learn-marketing-attribution-with-phoebe).
**Arc:** Running-project - the builder track builds ONE launch campaign for **"Cadence"**, a fictional AI note-taker SaaS with self-serve (B2C-style PLG) + team/enterprise (B2B) motions, so both funnels are taught on one artifact. Two-track (a1-a6 leader, b1-b10 builder).
**Coverage bar:** each session teaches ~80% of its mapped sources' working content; certificates/paid tooling stay official.
**Build mode:** course-taking loop PAUSED (built direct from verified sources, no learner-notes step).

## Market context (verify before delivery - moves fast)

- 2026 AI-marketing curricula converge on the same spine: research -> positioning -> content -> SEO/GEO -> ads -> email -> social -> analytics -> agentic campaign planning.
- GEO (generative engine optimization / answer-engine optimization) is now a first-class topic alongside SEO as AI search overviews reshape discovery.
- Agentic marketing (autonomous campaign planning/execution) is emerging - taught as governed future, not turnkey today.

## Hard rails (bake into every relevant page)

- **"AI assists, the human owns the brand and the claim."** No fabricated stats, fake reviews, or unverifiable claims.
- **Disclosure rail:** FTC/ASA - disclose AI-generated endorsements/testimonials and material connections; do not deceive. SEC-style "AI-washing" of your own capabilities also applies to martech vendors.
- **Data-privacy rail:** personalization uses consented first-party data; respect GDPR/PDPA; no scraping PII into consumer tools.
- **IP/rights rail:** AI images/voices can raise copyright + likeness issues; use rights-clear inputs, review outputs.
