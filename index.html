Launch checklist
easy - everyone medium hands-on / deciding hardest
The one rule this whole course runs on: AI assists, you own the brand and the claim. No fabricated stats or fake reviews, disclose AI and endorsements where required (FTC/ASA), and personalize only with consented data. Credibility is the moat AI cannot generate for you.
